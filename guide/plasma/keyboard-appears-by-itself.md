## What you see

On a computer with a touchscreen, the on-screen keyboard comes up although you
did not tap a text box. It often happens just after you touched the screen,
when a new window opens or you switch to another tab.

## Why

When a program puts its cursor in a text box by itself, soon after you touched
the screen, the desktop takes that as a wish to type and brings up the keys.
Windows' touch keyboard waits for a tap on the box, and this desktop doesn't
always. Nothing is wrong with your computer.

## What to do

1. Put the keyboard away with its own key for hiding it.
2. Carry on. Nothing was typed.
