## What you see

After a few wrong passwords, at the lock screen or when you sign in, the
computer refuses your password even when you type it correctly. A message says
to wait.

## Why

It is a safety lock, like Windows locking an account after too many wrong PINs.
After a few wrong tries in a short time, the computer turns every try away for a
while, so that nobody can guess their way in. On many computers that is three
wrong tries within fifteen minutes, and a wait of ten minutes. Nothing is broken
and nothing is lost.

After each wrong password the lock also pauses for a moment, and a try started
during that pause is not heard at all.

## What to do

1. Stop trying for now.
2. Read the message on the screen. If it says the account is locked, wait ten
   minutes before the next try.
3. Check that Caps Lock is off, then type your password once, slowly.
