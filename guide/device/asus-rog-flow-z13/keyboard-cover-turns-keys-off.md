## What you see

With the keyboard cover attached, the on-screen keyboard no longer comes up,
anywhere, even on the lock screen.

## Why

With the cover attached, this tablet switches to laptop mode, like a Windows
2-in-1 that stops offering the touch keyboard once a keyboard is clipped on. In
laptop mode the on-screen keys are turned off everywhere. This is on purpose,
not a fault.

## What to do

1. Type on the keyboard cover.
2. Or take the cover off. The on-screen keyboard comes back when you tap a text
   box.
