# Roadmap

What is planned for Split Rock, in order, and what is not.

## First: the maintainer's own machine

- A panel widget that is an Agent Client Protocol client, starting with Claude
  Agent.
- The notebook: a chosen folder, shown in the widget.
- Explain and walk through: paste an error or describe a problem, get plain
  steps. Nothing runs.
- `This computer.md`: every change made by hand or by the assistant, with its
  undo. A real hardware troubleshooting history is the first test.

## Next: safe to hand to someone else

- The Do it card, with Show me how instead and Not now.
- A snapshot before every system change, where the disk supports it.
- Administrator rights through KDE's own prompt, one action at a time.
- Setup in three screens: choose an assistant, choose a notebook folder, choose
  what the assistant may touch.
- The built-in desktop guide that works with no account.

## Then: everyday help

- Text size, Wi-Fi, printers, updates, finding a download, installing an app.
- Scam checks: "is this real?" for emails, pop-ups and calls.
- Send this to someone: a plain summary of the problem and what was tried.

## Later

- Scenes: "get me ready for work" opens and arranges a saved set of
  applications. Needs a request interface in the window manager; on Shuffle,
  Kadunce decides and arranges.
- Voice: speaking to the assistant, and answers read aloud.
- Seeing the screen: "what is this window?", with permission each time.

## Not planned

- An AI model of Split Rock's own.
- Handling anyone's account, payment or password.
- Acting without a card.

## Open decisions

- The consumer name. Inside Shuffle it is the workspace assistant for now.
- Which agent setup offers first to someone with no account.
- Whether anything small, such as text size, may change without a card.
