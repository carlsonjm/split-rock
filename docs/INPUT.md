# Input

Every tap and key a Helper answers, and what you see happen. Each Helper opens
on its own, in an ordinary window, from Core or Search (`DESKTOP.md`).

| Task | Touch | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open a Helper | Tap it in Search | None yet |
| Act on a Helper | Tap its button | None yet |
| Undo a past change | Tap Undo beside a change on What changed | None yet |
| Scroll | Drag the Helper | None yet |
| Close | Tap Close or Not now, or close the window | The window's own |

## Open a Helper

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click it in Search, or tap or click a notice from Core | The Helper opens in its window and looks first: Update, Free up space, Install a new app, Remove an app, Health check, What changed, Restore points or Community apps. It changes nothing until its own button is tapped, and asks for the password in the system's own prompt where it must. |

## Act on a Helper

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click its button | The Helper does its one job, as its card said: what it changes is written in the notebook first, with how to undo it, and its progress shows on the card until it is done. |

## Scroll

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Drag the Helper with a finger or on a touchpad | It moves with the finger and carries on as usual. |
| Turn the mouse wheel | Each notch moves it a steady 80 pixels, eased, at Plasma's usual three lines a notch, however the mouse reports a notch. |

## Close

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click Close or Not now, or close the window | The Helper closes, and its window with it. A Helper changing something can't be closed until it is done. |

## Undo a past change

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click Undo beside a change, or Undo it, on What changed | That change is put back, the way its card promised, and its line in This computer.md says it was undone. A file changed since the change is left as it is, and nothing is put back; any file Undo replaces or removes is kept aside in the change's own folder first. Undo is offered only while nothing else is being changed. |
