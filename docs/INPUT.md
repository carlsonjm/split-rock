# Input

Every tap and key a Helper answers, and what you see happen. Each Helper opens
on its own, in an ordinary window, from Core or Search (`DESKTOP.md`).

| Task | Touch | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open a Helper | Tap it in Search | None yet |
| Act on a Helper | Tap its button | None yet |
| Undo a past change | Tap Undo beside a change on What changed | None yet |
| Scroll | Drag the Helper | None yet |
| Close | Tap Close or Not now, or close the window | The window's own |

## Open a Helper

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click it in Search, or tap or click a notice from Core | The Helper opens in its window and looks first: Update, Free up space, Install a new app, Remove an app, Health check, What changed, Restore points or Community apps. It changes nothing until you tap its own button, and asks for a password only in the system's own prompt, where one is needed. |

## Act on a Helper

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click its button | The Helper does its one job, as its card said. It first writes what it will change in the notebook, with how to undo it, and shows its progress on the card until it is done. |

## Scroll

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Drag the Helper with a finger or on a touchpad | It moves with the finger and carries on as usual. |
| Turn the mouse wheel | Each notch moves it a steady 80 pixels, eased, at Plasma's usual three lines a notch, however the mouse reports a notch. |

## Close

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click Close or Not now, or close the window | The Helper closes, and its window with it. A Helper changing something can't be closed until it is done. |

## Undo a past change

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click Undo beside a change, or Undo it, on What changed | Split Rock puts that change back, as its card promised, and marks its line in This computer.md as undone. If a file has changed since, Split Rock leaves it as it is and puts nothing back. Before Undo replaces or removes a file, it keeps a copy in the change's own folder. Undo is offered only while nothing else is being changed. |
