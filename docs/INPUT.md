# Input

Every tap and key Split Rock answers, and what you see happen. A panel widget
of Split Rock's own would add its own (`ROADMAP.md`).

Split Rock's icon opens the full window, an ordinary window. Ask, in the icon's
menu, opens the card. Inside Shuffle, Search also hosts the conversation as one
of its modes, drawn in Search's own window (`DESKTOP.md`).

The card is one card the size of Shuffle's Search, centred over the work: the
name on the left of its header, the question in the middle, Expand on the right,
and the answer below. In Spread it holds the centre, where Cards puts it. With
the on-screen keys up the card rises only as far as it must to stay clear of
them, then gets shorter. Expand hands over to the full window, an ordinary
window with the full view. There is no Back: Back
belongs to Search, and this is the assistant's own window.

## Open the assistant

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click its icon, in a launcher, in Search, on a dock or pinned to the panel | The full window opens: an ordinary window, which can be resized or placed beside another. With Split Rock already running, its window comes forward on the same conversation. |

## Open the card

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click Ask in its icon's menu | The card opens: in Spread it holds the centre; otherwise it stands centred over the work. |

## Choose an assistant

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click the assistant's name under the card's answer, or at the top of the full window, then one in the list | That assistant starts. Choosing another ends the conversation, which is not saved. |
| Tap or click Back in the list | The list closes, and the same assistant carries on. |

## Download the model

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click Download, after choosing Split Rock on this computer | The model, about 2.5 GB, downloads once, with its progress; the card names the model, who made it and its licence. Pause stops it, and Download picks up where it stopped. Once it is checked, the assistant starts. Not now goes back to the list of assistants. |

## Sign in

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click a way to sign in | The full window opens first, then the assistant opens its own sign-in page, usually in the browser, which can come in front of it. The window waits, and never sees the password. Cancel stops waiting. |

## Ask

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Type in the question field, then tap or click Send | The answer arrives below the header: one short line, then steps. The question stays in the field. |
| `Enter` in the field | The same as Send. |

## Open an upkeep card

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click its tile, under Look after this computer before anything is asked, or in This Device | The card opens in the conversation and looks first: Check for updates, Free up space, Install an app, Health check, What changed or Community apps. It changes nothing until its own button is tapped, and asks for the password in the system's own prompt where it must. |
| Tap or click a tile above the card's conversation | A tile says what Split Rock noticed, such as updates waiting, and opens the card that helps, or asks about it. With nothing to notice, it offers a card or opens an app the computer already has. |

## Take the tour

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click Show me around, with the guide chosen, which is offered only where Split Rock on this computer can't run | The first tour opens: what it shows, then its steps. A desktop's own tours come first. |
| Tap or click Next | The next tour opens, until the last. |

## Stop an answer

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click Send while it shows a square | The assistant stops; what it said so far stays. |

## Approve a change

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click Do it on its card | Its undo is recorded first, then only that change is made. A change to the system asks for the password in the system's own prompt. The button reads Making it… until the change is done, then the card closes and the assistant is told how it went. Do it is unavailable for a change the assistant tried to make itself, which was refused. |

## Decline a change

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click Not now on its card | Nothing changes, and the card is set aside. The answer keeps a Do it button that brings it back. |

## Learn instead

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click Show me how instead on its card | Nothing changes. The steps to make the change yourself appear, one at a time. |

## Pass to Codex

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click Ask Codex under the answer, on the card | Split Rock on this computer offers it when a change is bigger than it can make by itself. Codex starts, signing in first if it must, and the same question is asked of it. |

## Expand

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click Expand | The card grows to fill the work, leaving a narrow gutter, then the full window takes its place: the conversation so far on the left, and This Device on the right. Inside Shuffle the full window is the Active card. There is no way back to the small card; close the full window instead. |

## Open This Device

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click This Device, at the top of the full window | The drawer opens at the right: the upkeep tiles first, then System, About me, Troubleshooting and Changes, each folded until tapped. An upkeep card open in the conversation brings its own part to the top, open. Tap its fold button to put the drawer away. |

## Scroll

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Drag the conversation, or This Device, with a finger or on a touchpad | It moves with the finger and carries on as usual. |
| Turn the mouse wheel | Each notch moves it a steady 80 pixels, eased, at Plasma's usual three lines a notch, however the mouse reports a notch. |

## Close

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click beside the card | The card closes. The conversation stays with Split Rock, for its window and for Search, until Start over or the end of the session; it is never saved. |
| `Esc` | The same. |

## Undo a past change

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click Undo beside a change under Changes in This Device, or Undo it on the What changed card | That change is put back, the way its card promised, and its line in This computer.md says it was undone. A system change asks for the password again. Undo is offered only while nothing else is being changed. |

## Keep an answer

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click Keep this under it | The answer becomes a note, filed by topic in the notebook's `Notes/` folder, and the button reads Kept. An answer with a password, a card number, bank details or a code in it is not kept, and a line says why. |

## Remember something

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click Remember under "Remember that?" | The sentence is written into `About me.md`, dated, and the line goes. The assistant offers it; Split Rock asks, and writes only after this tap. |
| Tap or click Not now | Nothing is written, and the line goes. |

## Record a fix

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click That fixed it under the answer | The problem, in the words the conversation began with, and what fixed it go at the top of the fixed problems in `This computer.md`, dated, and the button reads Fixed. |

## Start over

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click the cross in the question field, once the question is answered | The conversation ends and is not saved, and the field is ready for a new question. Kept notes and the notebook stay. |
