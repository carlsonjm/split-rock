Windows called them virtual desktops, in Task View, and so does this desktop:
separate screens of windows, so that work and home can each have their own.

1. **Swipe sideways with three fingers on the screen.** You move to the virtual
   desktop beside this one, with its own windows. This works once you have more
   than one.
